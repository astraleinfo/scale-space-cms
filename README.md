# Scale Space Builders — Construction Management (demo)

Static, frontend-only demo. No build step and no backend.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

```
python3 -m http.server 8080   # then visit http://localhost:8080
```

- **Sign-in screen:** pick a role (Owner, Admin, Project Manager, Site Engineer, Supervisor, Accounts or Office Admin). You can switch roles at any time from the "Viewing as" pill.
- **Data:** sample data is generated relative to today. Changes are saved in `localStorage` for the day. Use *Reset demo data* in the user menu to start fresh.
- **Floors:** each project has levels (foundation, floors, terrace — or zones for interiors) with their own activities. Overall project progress is the average of all activities. Daily report work items can be linked to a floor activity to update it.
- **Daily reports:** workforce, wages and payroll come from the crew recorded on each day's report. Reports are due by 8:00 PM on working days (Mon–Sat) with at least one photo; each site-day is Submitted, Late, Pending or Missing (*Site Operations › Submission status*). Supervisors can ask the office to upload a missed report; the Office Admin uploads it on their behalf, and the uploader, actual upload time, late reason and later corrections are recorded.
- **Attendance:** staff attendance (every employee except the owner) with a selfie check-in and check-out and optional GPS check. The camera uses `getUserMedia` where allowed, with a fallback to upload or a sample selfie. *Simulate on-site location* is a demo shortcut. GPS points are shown on a map (OpenStreetMap via Leaflet, loaded only when needed) with the site's 300 m check-in radius, in the attendance detail, the check-in form and a *Check-in locations* map for managers on the Today and Daily tabs. Each point shows its place name and street address. Addresses are looked up with OpenStreetMap's Nominatim, one at a time, and cached in the browser. Without internet the site's registered address and a Google Maps link are shown instead. Worker (labour) attendance still comes from daily reports.
- **Notifications** (bell icon) are in-app only; nothing is emailed or texted.
- **Print / PDF** uses the browser's print dialog (choose *Save as PDF*); *Download* saves a standalone HTML copy.
- **Settings › Dropdown values** (Owner and Office Admin; Project Managers can view): edit the lists used in forms and filters, such as stoppage reasons, leave types and allowances, the material catalogue, GST rates, payment modes, expense categories, enquiry sources and document categories. You can add, rename, reorder or remove values. Renaming also updates the records that use the value. Values the app's logic depends on are locked. Status lists (project, invoice, document, attendance statuses and roles) are not editable. Settings are kept in browser storage apart from the demo data, so they survive the daily reseed and *Reset demo data*; each list has *Restore defaults*.
- **Invite / edit user:** profile photo, mobile, an alternate contact (number, name and relationship), Aadhaar number (checked with the Aadhaar check digit, shown masked as XXXX XXXX 1234) with a photo or PDF of the card, address (with state and PIN), and a role dropdown that shows what each role can access. Click a user to see their profile. Only people who manage users (and the user themselves) can see Aadhaar and address details. In the demo, photos and cards are shrunk and kept in browser storage.
- **Salary, bonus & last appraisal** (Performance › employee page, Performance › *Salary & bonus* tab, user profile): yearly salary history (CTC, monthly gross, increment %, reason, appraisal rating) with a chart by financial year, bonuses (festival, performance, project completion…), *Revise salary* (suggested increment from the last appraisal rating), *Add bonus*, printable revision letters, and a *Last appraisal* card (rating, metric scores, strengths, areas to improve, goals). Salaries are financial: the Owner and Accounts (*Manage salaries & bonuses*) see and change everyone's; each employee sees only their own, read-only; the Admin role sees no one else's salary.
- **Analytics** (Owner): opens with *Key takeaways* written in plain language (projects at loss risk, schedule slips, net cash, unpaid billing, main stoppage cause, win rate), headline cards with trends, then numbered sections that each explain what they show: cash flow (collected vs spent with a net line), project profitability (spent, estimated final cost and contract value per project; chart or table), schedule (planned vs actual), workforce & stoppages, and sales (funnel with stage-to-stage conversion, lead sources by outcome).
- **Themed dropdowns and date pickers:** on mouse/trackpad devices every dropdown and date field opens a popover in the app's own style (light and dark), with search for long lists, keyboard support, min/max dates, *Today* and *Clear*. Touch devices keep the phone's own pickers.
- **Stoppage day-by-day log:** each stoppage has a main reason (with a free-text reason when *Other* is chosen), from and to dates, details of why work stopped, and a row for every working day in the period: work done (no work / half day worked / worked), the reason that day, and a comment on why there was no work. Days lost are counted from these rows (Sundays are not counted). Ongoing stoppages have *Log today* for a quick daily entry.
- **Document control:** documents are filed by type (floor plans, elevations, architectural, structural, electrical, plumbing, approvals, contracts, reports, invoices, other). An upload needs a title, type, floor / area, version number, upload date and description; a duplicate title in the same project is refused so revisions stay in one history. Statuses: Draft, Under Review, Approved, Finalized, Rejected, Superseded, Archived. Every card and the preview say plainly what the site should use (“Use v3 · Finalized”); opening an older, draft or rejected version shows a red *Do not use on site* banner and a watermark, and downloading it asks first. Reviewers can *Mark as final* (issued for construction). Revisions add versions; documents are archived, never deleted.
- **Project › Documents:** documents grouped by category (drawing types first, then approvals, contracts, reports…), with counts and dots for finalized / not-approved items. The open category lists documents newest upload first, each with title, version, status, floor / area, upload date and uploader, what the site should use (e.g. *Latest final: v2*), Preview, *Download* of the version in use, and a fold-out of earlier versions to view or download. Archived documents can be shown with a tick box.
- **Project notes** (Project › Notes): title, type (instruction, client discussion, design change, site observation, other), description, author, created and last-updated date (and who updated it). Pin, edit and delete for the author, project manager, Owner and Admin; read-only for others who can see the project. Instructions and design changes notify the site team.
- **Agreements** (Owner, Project Manager, Accounts, Office Admin; hidden from roles without financial access): Tamil building contract agreements (கட்டிட ஒப்பந்தப் பத்திரம்) made from the company template (*FINAL AGREMENT 07-03-2026*). Four steps:
  1. *Fill in details*: date, building owner (name, relation, father / husband, Aadhaar with check-digit validation, place), site address, the floors being built (ground and first floor to start with; *Add floor* adds a second, third… floor and each can be renamed in English and Tamil or removed), each floor's area and rate (amounts are calculated; page 1, the page 2 area and cost lines and the work progress heading list every floor), special works with their Tamil description for page 1, payment schedule (*Put the balance in the last stage*), the contractor's details (pre-filled), and every number, size, type, material and brand: the specification tables (work progress, specification, doors, windows, electricals, plumbing, tile, painting, exclusions, materials) each in their own section, pre-filled from the template, with pick-or-type suggestions from the template and earlier agreements; and the water tank (number, material, capacity). A list of the form's sections down the left side (a scrolling strip on phones) jumps straight to any section, highlights the one being read and marks sections with details still missing; the form uses the rest of the width.
  2. *Terms & conditions*: the template's 13 terms with their standards, materials and amounts (quality standard, door, handrail and waterproofing materials, kitchen work, the two amounts) as boxes inside the fixed wording; *Add a term* for your own terms (with *Remove*). Every term can be dragged by its handle to a new place — with a mouse or on a touch screen, or with the arrow keys (Home / End) on a focused handle; Escape cancels a drag. Terms are numbered in the order shown and each template term keeps a *Template n* tag. If the template's own terms are moved out of their order, *Restore template order* puts them back (keeping added terms where they were) and Verify shows a warning. The template's wording is not changed.
  3. *Verify*: checks (missing details, Aadhaar, totals, payment schedule = agreement value, empty added terms, page 1 fits) and the key details to compare with the owner's documents; tick the confirmation and *Finalize*. Final agreements are read-only; the Owner can reopen one as a draft.
  4. *Download PDF*: an A4 PDF in Tamil with the letterhead on every page and both parties' signature lines on page 1 and the last page (no company seal). Pages are drawn by the browser (html2canvas + jsPDF from cdnjs, loaded on first use), so Tamil conjuncts and vowel signs match the preview exactly; *Print* gives a text PDF via *Save as PDF*.
  - *Live preview* (every step): closed by default; *Show live preview* (or the floating *Live preview* button) opens the A4 pages beside the form, or along the bottom on narrow screens, and they update as you type. Focusing a field highlights its value. It closes again when you open another agreement.
  - The template's wording and the 13 terms are fixed apart from the boxes above. Agreements are kept in browser storage apart from the demo data, so the daily reseed and *Reset demo data* don't remove them. The signed agreement of 01.03.2026 is included as a sample (with the parties' real Aadhaar numbers from that document).
- **Performance is Owner-only:** scores, appraisals and the Salary & bonus tab are visible only to the Owner. The module is locked to *No access* for every other role in Roles & permissions (marked *Owner only*), dashboard links and notifications that point to it are hidden for other roles, and older saved permission settings cannot reopen it.
- **Admin role** (Ramesh Iyer): manages users, roles, access permissions, settings, projects and every operational module (site reports, attendance, leave, performance, materials, service, documents, enquiries), but has **no financial access**.
  - Payments & Expenses, Invoices & Bills and Analytics are hidden, and blocked even when opened by URL.
  - Amounts, wages, budgets, quotations, purchase prices, bill status and financial reports and exports are removed from every other screen.
  - As a safety net, every money figure is masked (`₹ •••`) for any role without *View financial details*.
- **Roles & permissions** (*Users & Access*) are editable by the Owner and Admin. Only the Owner can change financial modules and actions (marked *Financial*) or assign roles with financial access; nobody can change their own role's access; the Owner always keeps full access. Changes are kept in browser storage apart from the demo data.
- **Security note:** this is a front-end demo with all data in the browser, so these rules are enforced in the interface only. A real deployment must also enforce them on the server.
- **Sample photos:** every photo field has *Add a sample photo* so flows can be demonstrated without real images. Generated material, defect and selfie images are labelled *Sample*.
- **Site media:** sample photos and walkthrough videos live in `assets/media/` (videos ship as WebM + MP4). Regenerate them with `python3 tools/generate_media.py` (needs Pillow and ffmpeg).
- **Uploads:** uploaded files are previewed in the browser and kept only until the page reloads.
